Profiles

Everyone signed in sees every profile. Tap a colour to change it.

Trading options

The dropdowns on the trading form. Adding your first one to a list copies the defaults across, so nothing disappears from an old trade.

Finance categories

The colour is the category’s slice in the Finances chart. Click a swatch on any row to change it.

Greetings

The line under the heading on each page. Each profile picks its own style above, in the profiles list. Write {name} where the profile name should appear.

Currency

Trades are entered and stored in USD. Finances are entered and stored in MAD. Neither is convertible on the way in — this rate only changes what the top-bar toggle shows you.
